Privacy Policy — Manga Rain

Last updated: 5 August 2026

Manga Rain reads comics and books aloud on your phone. This policy says exactly

what stays on your device, what leaves it, and who sees it.

There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of

any kind built into this app. Your library, your reading position and everything

the app has read aloud stay on your phone.

Reading a page aloud happens entirely on your device

When Manga Rain reads a comic, it looks at the page to find the lettering,

decides what is speech and what is narration, and synthesises the voice — all

locally. Page images are never uploaded anywhere. The text-recognition model

is bundled inside the app itself, and the voices are files on your phone. Turn

off the network after a chapter has loaded and read-along still works.

The same is true of books: the text is processed on your device.

What actually leaves your device

1. Performance metrics from Google's text recognition. Manga Rain uses

Google ML Kit to recognise lettering on a page. ML Kit sends Google metrics

about the performance and utilisation of those APIs — things like whether a

recognition call succeeded and how long it took. This is diagnostic information

about the software, not about you, and it **does not include the page images or

the recognised text**. We are required to tell you this and we have no way to

switch it off. Google's handling of that data is covered by the

Google Privacy Policy.

This is the only telemetry in the app. Nothing else reports anything, anywhere.

2. Requests to the sites your content comes from. When you browse or read,

the app fetches pages and images directly from whichever source you chose. Those

sites receive the ordinary things any website receives when your phone asks it

for something: your IP address, what you requested, and your device's user-agent

string. Manga Rain does not add anything to those requests, and it does not

report your reading back to us — there is no "us" to report to. Each of those

sites has its own privacy practices, which we neither control nor endorse.

The sources the app can contact are: MangaDex, Asura Scans, Weeb Central,

MangaPill, MangaKatana, FindNovel, DaisyNovel, Royal Road, Wattpad, and Project

Gutenberg.

3. Downloading a voice. If you choose a neural voice, the model file is

downloaded once from Hugging Face or GitHub. That is a plain file download; the

host sees the request.

4. Checking for an app update. On launch the app fetches a small file from

GitHub to see whether a newer version exists. GitHub sees that request.

What is stored, and where

Everything below is on your phone, in the app's own storage, and goes nowhere

else:

  • your library, reading progress and the position you stopped at
  • what the app worked out a page says, so a re-read is instant
  • any corrections or voice assignments you make
  • pages and chapters you saved for offline reading
  • your settings

Uninstalling the app removes all of it. Backups produce a file you control —

where you put it, and who can then read it, is up to you.

Permissions

  • Internet — to fetch what you read, download a voice, and check for updates.
  • Notifications and foreground service — to keep narration playing with the

screen off and show the player in your notification shade.

  • Wake lock — to stop the phone sleeping mid-chapter while it reads to you.
  • Storage — to save chapters for offline reading and to write backups.
  • Install packages — to apply an app update you have accepted.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or your phone's

identifiers, and does not read anything outside its own storage except files you

explicitly point it at.

Children

Manga Rain is not directed at children and collects nothing that could identify

anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the version in

the app's Settings always links to the current one.
